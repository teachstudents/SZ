Vega, Shenzhen

Reviewed yesterday ★★★☆☆ (3 out of 5 stars) Great mains, pity about the service

I came here with my sister for lunch. It is a Thai restaurant located at Coco Park.

The main dishes were amazing! The Tom yum is truly authentic, making me feel I'm dining in Bangkok already! Its spicy and sour flavour is absolutely delicious and I love the balance of flavors! Aside from Tom yum, I highly recommend trying this mouth-watering dish: Grilled Pork Neck. Its crispy skin and tender meat are super-duper tasty! All dishes we had were full of flavor with reasonable price. We only spent 60 dollars per person for 5 dishes!!

Another thing that I really like is the decoration of the restaurant. The restaurant has a tropical theme with giant leaves and flowers hanging from the ceiling. What surprises me the most was that all waiters were wearing traditional Thai costumes and they greeted us in Thai! However, the service was below average. When we arrived at 1:40 PM, we had to wait 20 minutes to get a table. What a waste of time! We also ordered chips as a side dish. At first, they looked yummy, but when we tasted them, they were burnt and too oily. When we told the waiter that we didn't like the chips, he just said, "That's funny, I love them," and didn't do anything else. We didn't even get an apology!

I was really excited about visiting Vega, and the main courses were just fantastic, but the rest of the experience was really upsetting. Therefore, I will only rate it 3 stars.

A Taste of Italy at Bella Pizza!

Bella Pizza, Sea World Last Saturday with my family

Last Saturday, I visited Bella Pizza located in Sea World with my family. It is a fantastic Italian restaurant.

We ordered the Margherita Pizza and Spaghetti Bolognese. The pizza was baked to perfection! It had a crispy crust and mouth-watering melted cheese. The spaghetti was also delicious, with a rich and savory tomato sauce. For $80, the meal was very reasonable and a great bargain!

The restaurant had a cheerful atmosphere, with beautiful Italian paintings on the walls and soft music. The staff were very attentive and brought our food promptly. Although the dining room was a little cramped because it was so popular, we still had a wonderful time.

Overall, I highly recommend this amazing restaurant to anyone who loves authentic Italian food. I rate it 5 out of 5 stars!
